## What changed

The opt-in decoder has five hidden states: home-young, home-established, away-young, away-established and dead. Young/established are latent possession-age phases, not additional output labels. Their probabilities collapse into the existing H/A/D contract, and X abstention handling still uses those collapsed posteriors. Both forward-backward and Viterbi operate on the same five-state model.

Age phases produce a non-geometric team-possession duration distribution. Young possessions have H/A exit probability fixed at 0.1 times the original team-switch probability, while progression and established-possession exit use `1-exp(-2/(scale_seconds*fps))`. The two-phase structure and 0.1 factor are modelling choices fixed before evaluation, **not learned optima**. This is a compact phase-type duration model, not a general nonparametric semi-Markov model or an exact elapsed-time counter.

There is no hard minimum duration or maximum duration: young possessions can end, and the established phase has a geometric self-loop allowing long possessions. Original ownership emissions are duplicated across their corresponding phases, without changing the evidence or enabling any Steps 2–5 options. Each phase retains its original team-to-D probability; D exits reset the corresponding team to its young phase. D predictions can nevertheless change because sequence context changes. Half boundaries reset inference independently, and outside-period labels stay X.

## How duration was estimated

The scale is the equal-match average of mean complete H/A reference-spell durations, pooling teams symmetrically. Reference spells touching either edge of a known-label run are excluded because their complete length is unknown. For every development match, its scale is estimated from the other eleven development matches only. Its own reference is used solely for evaluation. No comparison-match or public labels enter fitting.

The final candidate uses all twelve development matches, yielding a scale of **9.512913 seconds**. This is a method-of-moments-style empirical scale, not a guaranteed expected output duration or maximum-likelihood fit of every hazard. Censoring exclusions and provider labelling conventions can bias the estimate; H/A-only reference data cannot fit D durations, which remain geometric under the original transition settings. Exact fold assignments, scale estimates and sample counts are committed in the aggregate report.
